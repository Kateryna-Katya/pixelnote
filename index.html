X-Demon

You're staring down this creepy portal, and honestly? Someone's gotta hold the line.

An ancient altar has opened a passage to a darker dimension. Stone guardians stand between the world and the forces beyond. In X-Demon, the player steps into a tense fantasy setting where every action feels like part of a larger battle.

From the get-go, there's this oppressive vibe that just hits you. It's like something ancient and nasty is watching from every shadow, and the graphics don't let you forget it for a second.
